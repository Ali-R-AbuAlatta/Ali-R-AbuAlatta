## 🧩 Working Principles & Engineering Approach

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Business Before Code

- Start by understanding the real operational need.
- Avoid features without a clear business purpose.
- Translate policies and workflows into system behavior.
- Focus on practical solutions users can adopt.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Clear System Design

- Break complex systems into understandable modules.
- Define responsibilities and boundaries before implementation.
- Design data models around actual business rules.
- Avoid unnecessary technical complexity.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Secure & Reliable Delivery

- Treat authentication, permissions and data integrity as core requirements.
- Apply validation at application and database levels.
- Protect sensitive operations with clear controls.
- Use testing, reviews and incremental stabilization.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Incremental Improvement

- Establish a stable baseline before major changes.
- Deliver improvements in controlled, reviewable batches.
- Separate UI improvements from validated business logic.
- Document decisions, assumptions and constraints.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Collaboration & Communication

- Communicate technical decisions in clear business language.
- Coordinate with developers and non-technical stakeholders.
- Clarify requirements before implementation.
- Surface risks and blockers early.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Practical Use of AI

- Use AI to support analysis, planning, coding and review.
- Keep human validation over important decisions.
- Apply structured prompts and documented workflows.
- Use AI where it improves quality or efficiency.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

> I believe good software starts with understanding the operation, grows through clear technical decisions, and succeeds when it reliably serves real users.

```text
Business Understanding
        ↓
Requirements & System Design
        ↓
Incremental Development
        ↓
Testing & Technical Review
        ↓
Deployment & Stabilization
        ↓
Continuous Improvement
```
